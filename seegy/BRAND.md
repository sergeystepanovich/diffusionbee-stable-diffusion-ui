## Positioning

Atmospheric progressive / melodic techno with emotional drive — night energy, journey-like builds, festival-ready but not cheesy. Brand feels cinematic, dark-to-light, modern electronic — not meme EDM, not generic club flyer.

## Tone of voice

- Confident, concise, cinematic
- Prefer concrete release facts over hype adjectives
- Avoid: "banger", "lit", emoji spam, fake urgency, corporate fluff
- OK: "Out now", "Extended Mix", "on Interplay", festival/city names, collaborator credits
- CTAs: verb + outcome — "Stream on Spotify", "Get it on Beatport", "Listen to Flowave"

## Visual direction (covers / promo art)

- Mood: night skies, neon haze, geometric light, cosmic depth, fog, silhouettes, horizons
- Palette cues: deep navy / black, electric cyan, violet accents, warm amber highlights — not pastel, not purple-on-white SaaS look
- Typography on covers: bold condensed sans or clean modern geometric; artist name **SEEGY** must read as the hero brand signal
- Avoid: stock handshake photos, random meme collage, cluttered flyer text walls
